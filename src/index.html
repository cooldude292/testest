The flow is solved live on a 2D slice through the body with a lattice Boltzmann solver + LES turbulence model. 3D forces are estimated by applying the slice's coefficients to the body's real frontal and planform areas. Good for comparing shapes, angles and fluids. Not a substitute for a real CFD run or a real tunnel.
